About A research initiative and think tank

Our thesis: mechanobiology is a common cause of organismal aging on Earth and physiological deterioration in space. We use agentic AI and biomolecular tools across a pipeline aimed at healthy lifespan extension and resilience under extreme conditions.

Project The ECM Aging Atlas

A knowledge base of time-resolved matrisome signatures extracted from public proteomic datasets. It aggregates how extracellular matrix composition shifts with age, tissue by tissue.

full deck
Project The Spacefarer Phenome

An optimized set of human phenotypic traits adapted to the rigors of space exploration, and the genetics behind them. Our drug discovery pipeline translates protective genetic variation into interventions to promote astronaut resilience to cosmic radiation, microgravity, and circadian disruption.
